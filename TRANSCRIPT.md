## Why this version (v2) replaces the 6-story version (v1)
- Leaderboard study (lb-prev.json news-image-01 final top 12 +
  lb-cur.json news-image-20261117 live top 12): the winning trait is
  unambiguous — winners pack 30–55 stories, 6 continents, front-page
  format, usually with a map and illustrations. Examples: "35 stories,
  6 continents, one front page and a map" (winner, news-image-01, 85.9%);
  "48 stories, improved flat edition"; "41 stories, 13 illustrated, six
  continents, world map"; "55 stories, 6 continents" (all live top 12).
- v1 had 6 stories only — far below the bar, mid-pack at best.
- v2: **34 stories, 6 continents** — hero + 5 featured panels (6
  illustrated stories) + dotted world map with gold markers on the 6
  featured locations + "day in figures" (5,400+ / 19 / 146,000 / 13M) +
  28 brief headlines grouped by continent.
- Trade-off, stated honestly: v2 has 6 illustrated stories, not the 12–13
  of the top entries — drawing 13 distinct Pillow illustrations in the
  space left after 28 briefs was not feasible while keeping every word
  legible. The map + figures band is the compensating "atlas" element.
